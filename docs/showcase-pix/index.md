## English

Showcase Pix turns your iPhone, iPad or Apple TV into a digital photo frame for travel memories, family portraits and everyday moments. Bring together photos from your library and your own servers, choose how they play, and give a spare device a new job on your desk, bedside table or shelf, or put your photos on the biggest screen in the house.

Sort photos the way you like, include subfolders, and refresh sources automatically so new photos appear on their own. Network sources connect directly from your device to your servers.

### Time, date and weather

Turn on **Show time and date** to see the clock, date, weekday, current temperature and weather conditions while your photos play. Weather icons and animated backgrounds follow the conditions outside and refresh automatically. Weather uses only your approximate location, and the clock and date stay visible even when weather is unavailable.

### Every photo has more to tell

In Slideshow, Fade and Calendar, tap a photo to see the details it carries: capture time, camera and lens, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, focal length, image size, file size, and where it was taken when the photo includes a location. The overlay hides again after a moment.

Connect your own AI image service to add a description, a short caption and tags to each photo, written in the app's language. Summaries are saved for next time, and a double-tap creates a new one. Before any photo is sent, the app asks for your consent and shows the service's domain. By default, photos with faces are detected on your device and are not uploaded.

### On Apple TV

Showcase Pix also runs on Apple TV, so your photos can fill the TV in the living room. Navigate with the Siri Remote, and the screen stays on while photos play. Apple TV has no photo library, so it plays photos from your network sources, such as a NAS, WebDAV, S3-compatible storage, Immich or MT Photos. Set up sources on your iPhone or iPad and turn on iCloud Sync to use them on Apple TV without typing server details with the remote. Apple TV cannot check photos for faces, so with face privacy on, which is the default, AI image summaries send no photos from Apple TV.

### Privacy by design

- No account required.
- Your photos stay on your device or go directly to the servers you configure.
- On first launch, the app asks whether you want to share usage and crash data. Nothing is collected before you allow it.
- AI image summaries are optional and are never sent without your consent.
- Weather uses only approximate location, rounded to about 1 km.

### Requirements

- iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15.0 or later, or Apple TV with tvOS 16.0 or later.
- Weather requires an internet connection and location access.
- AI image summaries require your own compatible AI service and API key.

### 时间、日期与天气

开启 **显示时间和日期** 后，播放照片时即可查看时钟、日期、星期、当前温度和天气状况。天气图标与动态背景随天气变化，并会自动刷新。天气功能只使用您的大致位置；天气暂时无法获取时，时钟和日期仍会正常显示。

### 照片里的故事，不止于画面

在幻灯片、淡入淡出和日历模式中，轻点照片即可查看它保留的信息：拍摄时间、相机与镜头、光圈、快门速度、ISO、焦距、图片尺寸、文件大小；带有位置信息的照片还会显示拍摄地点。信息面板会在片刻后自动隐藏。

连接您自己的 AI 图片服务后，还可以为每张照片生成内容描述、简短文案和标签，语言跟随 App 当前语言。摘要会保存以便下次使用，双击即可重新生成。发送任何照片之前，App 都会征求您的同意，并显示该服务的域名。默认情况下，系统会在设备本地检测人脸，含人脸的照片不会上传。

### 在 Apple TV 上

Showcase Pix 同样可以在 Apple TV 上运行，让照片铺满客厅的电视。使用 Siri Remote 即可操作，播放期间屏幕保持常亮。Apple TV 没有照片图库，因此会播放来自网络图片源的照片，例如 NAS、WebDAV、S3 兼容存储、Immich 或 MT Photos。在 iPhone 或 iPad 上设置好图片源并开启 iCloud 同步，即可直接在 Apple TV 上使用，无需用遥控器输入服务器信息。Apple TV 无法检测照片中的人脸，因此在默认开启人脸隐私保护时，AI 图片摘要不会从 Apple TV 发送任何照片。

### 隐私优先

- 无需注册账号。
- 照片保存在您的设备上，或直接发送到您配置的服务器。
- 首次启动时会询问您是否共享使用与崩溃数据，您允许之前不会收集任何数据。
- AI 图片摘要为可选功能，未经您同意不会发送任何照片。
- 天气功能只使用取整到约 1 公里的大致位置。

### 系统要求

- 运行 iOS 或 iPadOS 15.0 及以上版本的 iPhone 或 iPad，或运行 tvOS 16.0 及以上版本的 Apple TV。
- 天气功能需要网络连接和位置权限。
- AI 图片摘要需要您自行配置兼容的 AI 服务和 API 密钥。
