## English

**Your photos deserve a place in your day.**

Showcase Pix turns your iPhone or iPad into a digital photo frame for travel memories, family portraits and everyday moments. Connect your photo sources, choose a layout you love, and give your favorite photos a place on your desk, bedside table or shelf.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6744004121)

### A new way to see your photos

Choose from slideshows with several transition effects, gentle fades, photo walls, scrolling waterfall layouts, Focus Photo Wall, Bento collages and photo calendars. Adjust playback intervals, transitions, image fitting and layout settings to find a pace that feels right.

### Your photos, from more places

Display pictures from your photo library, or connect to your own SMB shares, WebDAV servers and S3-compatible storage. Self-hosted photo services such as Immich and MT Photos, RSS feeds, and GitHub or Gitee repositories you choose are supported too. Network sources connect directly from your device to your servers.

### A fresh view of every day

See the time, date and local weather alongside your photos. Pair favorite images with a photo calendar to make your screen a useful part of your daily routine. The screen stays on while your photos are playing.

### Every photo has more to tell

In Slideshow, Fade and Calendar modes, view available photo details such as capture time, camera, lens and exposure settings. If you connect your own compatible AI image service, Showcase Pix can also generate descriptions, short captions and tags.

### Make it feel like you

Choose from multiple color themes and light or dark mode. Keep your settings and photo sources in sync across your Apple devices with iCloud.

### Privacy by design

- No account required.
- Your photos stay on your device or go directly to the servers you configure.
- On first launch, the app asks whether you want to share usage and crash data. Nothing is collected before you allow it.
- AI image summaries are optional. Before any photo is sent, the app asks for your consent and shows the domain of your AI service. By default, photos that contain faces are detected on your device and are not uploaded.
- Weather uses only approximate location, rounded to about 1 km.

### Requirements

- iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15.0 or later.
- Weather requires an internet connection and location access.
- AI image summaries require your own compatible AI service and API key.

## 中文

**把珍藏，变成每天看得见的风景。**

Showcase Pix 将 iPhone 和 iPad 变成数字相框，让旅行风景、家人合影与日常瞬间在屏幕上自然流转。连接喜欢的照片来源，选一种适合心情的展示方式，让回忆融入日常。

[在 App Store 下载](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6744004121)

### 多种展法，让照片各有主场

从带多种转场效果的幻灯片与柔和的淡入淡出，到照片墙、瀑布流、焦点照片墙、Bento 拼贴和照片日历，自由选择照片的呈现方式。调整播放间隔、转场、图片适配与布局，找到自己喜欢的观看节奏。

### 好照片，不止一个来源

展示照片图库中的珍藏，也能连接您自己的 SMB 共享、WebDAV 服务器和 S3 兼容存储。同时支持 Immich、MT Photos 等自建相册服务、RSS 订阅，以及您指定的 GitHub 或 Gitee 仓库。网络图片源由您的设备直接连接您的服务器。

### 每天，都有好风景

欣赏照片时，一眼查看时间、日期与当地天气。用照片日历装点书桌、床头或客厅，让屏幕成为日常空间里的一处风景。播放照片期间，屏幕会保持常亮。

### 照片里的故事，不止于画面

在幻灯片、淡入淡出和日历模式中，查看照片保留的拍摄时间、相机、镜头与曝光参数。连接您自己的兼容 AI 图片服务后，还可以生成图片描述、简短文案和标签。

### 把喜欢的色彩，带进相册

多款配色主题，搭配浅色或深色外观。通过 iCloud 在您的 Apple 设备之间同步设置和图片源。

### 隐私优先

- 无需注册账号。
- 照片保存在您的设备上，或直接发送到您配置的服务器。
- 首次启动时会询问您是否共享使用与崩溃数据，您允许之前不会收集任何数据。
- AI 图片摘要为可选功能。发送任何照片之前，App 都会征求您的同意，并显示 AI 服务的域名。默认情况下，系统会在设备本地检测人脸，含人脸的照片不会上传。
- 天气功能只使用取整到约 1 公里的大致位置。

### 系统要求

- 运行 iOS 或 iPadOS 15.0 及以上版本的 iPhone 或 iPad。
- 天气功能需要网络连接和位置权限。
- AI 图片摘要需要您自行配置兼容的 AI 服务和 API 密钥。
